About MNCollections

Created by the Minnesota Alliance of Local History Museums, MNCollections helps people discover historical records shared by organizations statewide.

Why local history matters

Photographs, maps and everyday objects connect Minnesotans with the people and places that shaped their communities. Making these records easier to discover helps keep local stories visible.

Local museums, shared stories

Local museums and historical societies preserve collections and choose which records they share for public discovery.

Places across Minnesota

Maps, photographs and records reveal how Minnesota towns, neighborhoods and landscapes have changed over time.

Objects with a story

Everyday artifacts and historic documents reveal the experiences of people and communities over time.
